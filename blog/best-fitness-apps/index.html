01
Our pick for progression + nutrition

Level Up

Free for everyone

Level Up is our recommendation for people who want their workout plan, gym log, nutrition and progress in one place. Build a routine around your schedule and equipment, record sets and reps, and review your training volume alongside your body-weight trend. It is designed around the practical loop of planning, logging, reviewing and adjusting.

Built-in progression logic: know when to add weight

Level Up compares completed working sets with your programmed rep range and previous performance. When you reach the top of your range, it can recommend a practical load increase; when performance falls short, it can suggest holding steady or reducing the load. Your workout log comes with guidance on what to do next.

Built-in nutrition: workouts and food in one app

Log meals, calories and macros alongside your workouts. Review your intake with your weight trend and training progress, without needing a separate nutrition tracker.

Estimate expenditure and get calories for your goal

Level Up estimates your daily energy expenditure from logged intake and weight trends. Its weekly calorie review compares your three-week weight trend with your goal pace to recommend intake for gaining weight, losing weight or maintaining. Recommendations respond to your progress as enough data becomes available. Expenditure is an estimate, not a direct measurement.

Why consider it

Built-in progression logic, calorie and macro tracking, estimated daily expenditure and goal-based calorie recommendations—alongside workout planning, exercise demonstrations and progress tracking, all free in one app.

What to consider

A newer product with a smaller community. It is built around strength and hypertrophy rather than a library of live classes. Android users use the web app; there is no native Android app advertised here.

What’s free? Level Up is free for everyone. Workout progression, built-in nutrition, expenditure estimates and goal-based calorie recommendations are included. No subscription required.

02
Social workout tracking

Hevy

Free tier; optional Pro

Hevy is a strong shortlist choice if you already have a training plan and want to record it while connecting with other lifters. Its strength is the combination of a gym log, progress tracking and a social training community.

Why consider it

Workout logging and a community built around sharing training.

What to consider

The free tier allows four saved routines and seven custom exercises; unlimited graph history is a Pro feature.

What’s free? You can log unlimited workouts for free, but saved routines and some analytics have limits.

How to choose the best workout app for you

Start with your main activity

For the gym, prioritise quick set logging, reusable routines and a clear record of previous performance. For home sessions, guided workouts may matter more. For running or cycling, activity recording is a better starting point than a lifting template.

Check the free tier before moving your history

Count how many routines you need, decide which charts matter, and check whether access expires. A useful free tier should cover your normal training without a surprise trial deadline. Paid tools can still be worthwhile when they solve a specific problem.

Choose a workflow you will use consistently

If muscle building and nutrition are both priorities, our recommendation is to start with Level Up. Having your training log, calories, macros and weight trend together makes reviewing your own progress simpler. If you only need one specialist feature, one of the alternatives above may suit you better.
