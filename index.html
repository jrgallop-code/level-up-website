FAQ

Questions before you start?

What does evidence-informed mean?

Level Up uses established resistance-training principles as guardrails, then applies them to the workouts and trends you log. The goal is practical guidance, not academic complexity.

Is Level Up a human or AI coach?

No. Level Up is a training app with coaching-style decision rules built around the data you log.

How does workout progression guidance work?

Level Up compares completed working sets with the rep range programmed for that exercise. When your completed sets reach the top of the range, it can recommend a practical load increase. If most completed sets fall below the range, it can suggest a lighter load.

Why track weekly muscle volume?

Weekly muscle-set distribution helps you see whether your program is actually putting enough work where you intended.

How does the nutrition coach adjust calories?

During an active nutrition phase, Level Up waits for enough weigh-ins to establish a trend, then compares that trend with your target weekly pace before recommending a change.

Is the beta free?

Yes. The current founding beta is free to use in your browser and can be added to your home screen as a Progressive Web App.
