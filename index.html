FAQ

Before your first session.

What is Level Up?

Level Up is a hypertrophy-focused training app for building workout programs, logging sessions, tracking progression, reviewing muscle volume and recovery, and keeping body-weight and nutrition targets in context.

Is the beta free?

Yes. The current founding beta is free to use.

Do I need the App Store or Google Play?

No. The beta runs as a Progressive Web App. Open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

Can Level Up build a workout plan for me?

Yes. Smart Builder can create a plan around your goal, schedule and priorities. You can also use templates or build a plan completely manually.

Can I still change exercises and sets?

Yes. Saved plans and workouts remain editable so you can adjust exercises, set counts, rep ranges and substitutions as your training changes.
