FAQ

Before your first workout.

What is Level Up?

Level Up is a hypertrophy-focused training app for building workout programs, logging sessions, tracking progression, and reviewing your training data.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. The beta runs as a Progressive Web App. Open it in your phone browser and add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

Is the beta free?

Yes. The founding beta is free to use while Level Up is being tested and improved.

Can I build my own workout program?

Yes. You can manually create a program, use templates, or use the guided Smart Builder to create a plan around your training goals.

Is Level Up only for advanced lifters?

No. It is designed to keep the logging experience straightforward while giving lifters deeper training context when they want it.
